A little about us

Made for the
in-between.

Fieldnote began with a simple thought: the best parts of a trip rarely happen at the famous viewpoint. They happen on the walk there, in the café you almost missed, or in a conversation that runs past sunset.

We make thoughtful travel inspiration for people who want to feel a place—not just see it. No packed itineraries, no pressure to do it all. Just practical ideas, local details, and a nudge to wander.
